A letter for you

Dear Sesi,

Happy birthday.

I do not have silver and gold to give you. If I did, I would give you all of it. What I have is this page and everything I feel for you, so I put the two together.

You have been there for me since I was small. I did not stay in your house, but you still looked after me like I was yours. You did the same for my sisters and our little brother. You tried, you did your best, and we all saw it. You were like a second mom to us.

You were the first person to take me to Joburg. Because of you I know Gauteng. You showed me a bigger world, and I walked into it because you went first.

Yes, sometimes you promised me things and could not keep them. I am not counting those. I have always known that the thought is what counts, and your thoughts always came from love.

I will never forget what you have done for me, my sisters and our little brother. Thank you for everything, Sesi. You deserve gold, you deserve silver, you deserve everything.

With all my love

Phuti
